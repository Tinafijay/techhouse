Meet the Tech House Editor: Accessible, AI-Assisted Video & Audio Editing

Tech House · A free, browser-based editor for everyone

Edit video and audio right in your browser — no installs, no accounts, no uploads to a server. Your files never leave your device, and an AI assistant does the heavy lifting so anyone can produce clean, well-paced media.

🎬 Open the Tech House Editor Free · Runs on your device · Works on desktop and mobile
A big win for the blind and visually impaired community. The editor was built accessibility-first: full keyboard control, screen-reader announcements for every action, live status updates, and an AI that can describe and transcribe your media for you. And because it is so simple, it is just as comfortable for sighted creators who want to move fast.

Why we built it

Most editors assume you can see a busy timeline and drag tiny clips with a mouse. That leaves a lot of people out — and slows everyone else down. We wanted an editor that you can drive entirely from the keyboard, that talks back to you, and that uses AI to handle the fiddly parts like transcription and pacing. The result runs 100% in your browser using a professional media engine (FFmpeg), so it is private by default.

What it does

  • Hybrid video & audio. Load a video or an audio file. The tools adapt to what you loaded — a full visual editor for video, and a focused sound editor for audio-only projects.
  • AI transcription, automatically. Powered by Gemini 3 Flash, the editor transcribes your audio into an editable transcript box every time you analyze — for video it also reads snapshots from the start, middle, and end for extra context.
  • One-click AI suggestions. Ask the AI to plan your edit, then press Apply Suggestions and it places illustrations, B-roll, background music, and sound effects — and sets their timing and volume — for you.
  • Auto-Cut Silence. It scans your audio and snips out the long, awkward pauses.
  • Layer stack. Add logos/watermarks, illustrations, background music playlists, sound effects, and B-roll overlays.
  • Accessible sound control. Nudge music and sound-effect timing with the keyboard, with the current time announced out loud as you go.
  • Private by design. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your media stays on your machine.
  • Export anywhere. Video projects export to MP4; audio projects export to MP3.

Built for accessibility

  • Full keyboard workflow — set in/out points, cut, zoom, add layers, play, and export without a mouse.
  • Screen-reader friendly: every action is announced, and status updates are read aloud.
  • Reduce-motion and high-contrast modes.
  • AI-generated transcripts double as captions material — great for accessibility and for repurposing content.

How to start in under a minute

  • Open the editor and drop in (or tap to choose) a video or audio file.
  • Add your Gemini API key in Editor Preferences, then press Analyze & Transcribe.
  • Review the transcript and suggestions, press Apply Suggestions, then Export.
